# Exploration

Before you explore, **talk with your Guide** about your intentions. You can attempt anything, and you automatically succeed at **reasonable or unchallenged actions**. For risky, uncertain, or dangerous actions, you must make a **Dice Check** or enter an **Encounter**.

## Dangerous Exploration

When exploring a dangerous area, the Guide sets:

- A number of **Danger Dice** (starting at 2)
- A **Danger Level** (between 1 and 5)

You take turns describing how you **search for treasure, traps, or secrets**.

If you collectively decide to actively investigate danger in your area or the next room—and there is only **one Danger Die**—you **find Danger**.

At this point, you may:

- Move first before a trap
- **Surprise** a group of enemies and start an encounter
- Or attempt to **elude the danger**, increasing the number of Danger Dice by 1

The Guide then secretly rolls the Danger Dice and removes any that are **at or below the Danger Level**. If no Danger Dice remain, **Danger finds you!**

- Traps trigger immediately
- Enemies gain **Surprise** and start an encounter

## Intervals

The time between encounters is counted in **Intervals**. During an Interval:

- You may face **traps, tense negotiations, or environmental hazards**, resolved with a **Dice Check**
- Each player may choose to roll **Pocket once** — a chance to steady themselves, shift their luck, or gain footing

An Interval ends when the group overcomes a significant risk, such as:

- Eluding an encounter
- Completing a tense negotiation
- Disarming a trap
- Or any similarly dangerous action

Afterward, the Guide begins the next Interval, giving players another opportunity to **roll Pocket**.
