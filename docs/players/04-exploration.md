# Exploration

Speak with your Guide about your intentions. You can attempt anything, and succeed automatically at anything reasonable. For uncertain or risky actions, make a **Dice Check** or enter an **Encounter**.

## Intervals

Each stretch of exploration is one **Interval**.

During an Interval, you may roll Pocket once — a single chance to steady yourself, shift your luck, or find your footing.

When enough risk has been overcome

- Surviving an encounter
- A tense negotiation
- Disarming a trap
- Or something equally dangerous

The Guide begins the next Interval, allowing another chance to reroll Pocket.

## Dungeon Procedure

When entering a dungeon the Guide decides on a number of d6 **Danger Dice** and a **Danger Level** between 1 and 5.

You can

- **Move** to an adjacent area.
- **Scout** for danger - the Guide reveals the current number of Danger Dice and Danger Level.
- **Explore** the area - describe how you search for treasure, traps, or secrets.

The Guide then secretly rolls the Danger Dice, removing any dice at or below the Danger Level. If no dice remain, an encounter occurs. If the last action was

- Move - the encounter starts as normal.
- Scout - the enemies are Surprised.
- Explore - the players are Surprised.
