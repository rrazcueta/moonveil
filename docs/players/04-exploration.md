# Exploration

Speak with your Guide about your intentions. You can attempt anything, and succeed automatically at anything reasonable. For uncertain or risky actions, make a **Dice Check** or enter an **Encounter**.

## Intervals

Each stretch of exploration is one **Interval**.

During an Interval, you may roll Pocket once — a single chance to steady yourself, shift your luck, or find your footing.

When enough risk has been overcome

- Surviving an encounter
- A tense negotiation
- Disarming a trap
- Or something equally dangerous

The Guide begins the next Interval, allowing another chance to reroll Pocket.
