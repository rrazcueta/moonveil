<!-- # Keywords

In the following sections for **Drafting Skills**, **Items**, and **Magic**, you’ll encounter **Keywords**.
These terms have specific meanings and are always used consistently in play.

- **Action** — A major effort taken during your turn, such as attacking, casting, or using an item.
- **Ability Point (AP)** — A limited personal resource you can mark to power skills, spells, or abilities.
- **Attacker** - The aggressor in an attack.
- **Cover** — Gain **Evade +1** while protected by terrain or obstacles. Does not stack.
- **Defender** - The **Target** of an attack.
- **Encounter** — Any structured moment of danger or conflict, including combat, traps, or tense negotiation.
- **Exploration** — The act of traveling, searching, and discovering between encounters.
- **Melee** — An attack made **0 Moves away** with a melee weapon.
- **Move** — A unit of distance or motion. Most characters can take one Move and one Action each round.
- **Near** — Within **0 Moves**; close enough to interact or act upon.
- **Spend** — Use **an Action or Move**, or **mark an Ability Point** to activate an effect.
- **Surprised** — One side acts **unopposed for the first round** of an encounter due to positioning or narrative advantage.
- **Target** — The **recipient** or **focus** of an Action or effect.
- **Unnoticed** — Temporarily **out of sight** or undetected; a state often exploited by rogues and scoundrels. -->
