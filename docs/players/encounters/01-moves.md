## Movement

Distances are measured by the number of Moves needed to cross them. 0 Moves means something is nearby and can be acted on immediately. 1 Move takes one Move to reach, 2 Moves takes two Moves, and so on.

### Obstacles

If an enemy blocks your path, roll DC10 Dexterity check to Pass:

- Roll with Advantage if the enemy is engaged with someone or something else.
- Roll with Disadvantage if multiple enemies block the way or the space is too narrow.
- On failure the blocking enemy gets a free attack.
- Or if the space is large enough, spend a Move to freely pass the enemy

If you need to navigate carefully—such as crawling over allies in a cramped space, avoiding a sprung trap, or moving across a slick surface—roll **Pocket Down**.

### Second Move

Spend your action and gain a second Move.

### Footing

Spend your Move to Pocket Roll. Mark Dexterity to reroll.
