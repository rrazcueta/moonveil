## Movement

Distances are measured by the number of Moves needed to cross them. 0 Moves means something is nearby and can be acted on immediately. 1 Move crosses a Distance of 2, 2 Moves crosses a Distance of 2, and so on.

Direction matters in a Melee as well. You protect whatever is behind you, and you can Move away in that direction as well. It costs 1 Move to reposition yourself in the same area. If you find yourself a junction, you will have to choose which side you defend.

- Spend a Move to cross a Distance of 1.
- Spend a Move to reposition yourself in the Melee.
- Spend a Move to gain better footing. Pocket Roll. Mark Dexterity to reroll.
- Spend an Action to gain another Move.

### Obstacles

If you're in a melee and want to move behind an enemy, roll DC10 Dexterity check to Pass:

- Roll with Advantage if the enemy is engaged with someone or something else.
- Roll with Disadvantage if multiple enemies block the way or the space is too narrow.
- On failure the blocking enemy gets a free attack.
- Or if the space is at least 1 Move wide, spend a second Move to reposition yourself behind the enemy. Then move again

If you need to navigate carefully—such as crawling over allies in a cramped space, avoiding a trap, or crossing a slick surface—roll **Pocket Down**.
