# Encounters

Encounters are dangerous situations where timing is essential. In a clash of steel or a sprung trap - each moment tests you and your fortitude. Dice roll, Pockets swing, and Ability Points are spent. When you are in a good position, be aggressive! When you are not, think quick! You may need to spend some resources or take a big risk - or risk losing your head.

## Initiative

Encounters are run in rounds. Start each round with a d6 **Initiative** roll to determine when you can act. On a 4–6 you act before the Guide; on a 1–3 you act after the Guide. You may reroll by marking Dexterity.

- **Opening Seekers**: if you rolled 4-6 take you can take your turn first
- **Guide**:
  - Enemies act
  - Hazards trigger
  - World events advance
- **Closing Seekers**: if you have yet to take your turn, take it here

On your turn, take a Move and an Action in any order alongside other Seekers taking their turns.
