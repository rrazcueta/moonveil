# Rest, Growth, and Death

## Rest

In a safe space, share a meal and spend 8 hours resting to recover.

### 1. Make a Record

You may choose to **Make a Record** — a note, sketch, meal, or other memento of your experience. **Spend a resource** like Dungeon Gear and

- Describe your creation to the table
- Place it in the **Extra** section of your Backpack
- Roll DC7 Insight to determine if it was successful

### 2. Level Up

If you reached a milestone

- Increase Level +1
- Gain +1 in an Ability Point

### 3. Recover

- **Determine HP**: Roll a number of d6 equal to your Level and add (3 × Strength). You can set your HP to this total.
- Recover Ability Points, Spells, Miracles, and lost HP
- Roll Pocket

## Death

At 0 HP you are dying. Only safety and long, sustained care can save you.

If this aid does not come, you are dead. Roll a new Seeker.
