# Items

Exploring the Moonveil means hauling gear — and sometimes hauling friends. Track everything you carry in **Equipment & Backpack**.

- **Slots**: Most items take up 1 slot. Larger items take up 2 or more slots.
- **Extra**: The smallest things like letters, keys, or coins are free to carry.

## Dungeon Gear

**Dungeon Gear** is a special item that takes 1 slot. When you need it, it becomes any reasonable adventuring tool: e.g. a club, a knife, arrows, rope, a tent, a torch, rations. For some kinds of items roll d6 to determine how much is available. For example, if you pull out rope you have d6 x 10 feet of rope; if you pull rations you have enough food and drink for d6 Seekers.

If you think you might need something, ask the Guide if they would allow it as Dungeon Gear. If they say no, ask if you can buy it and how much.

## Carrying Your Friends

Carrying a Seeker, without their gear, takes 5 slots. You need to accomodate slots for your own gear and any gear of theirs as well.

## Encumberance

You can carry 6 + Willpower items without penalty. For each item beyond that, you gain **Encumbrance**. Subtract your Encumbrance from every roll before resolving it.
