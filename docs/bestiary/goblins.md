# Goblins

Friends with dinosaurs. Sometimes skeletons.

## Goblin

Clubs, slings, kitchen knives. Annoying. Comes in pairs.

- Hit Points: d6
- Evade: 6
- Limit: 10
- Makeshift Bomb, Range 1.
  - Roll d6, on 6 it explodes immediately and everyone takes d6 damage.
  - On 2-5 it explodes next round.
  - On 1 it's a dud

## Tall Goblin

- Hit Points: 9 +d6
- Evade: 5
- Limit: 10
- Throwing Spear - Strong Attack +1 damage, Range 1
