# Animals

## Mountain Cat

Fast and evasive.

- Hit Points: 3 +d6
- Evade: 6
- Limit 10
- Seekers have to roll 5 or 6 to take their turns before Big Cat
- Sharp Claws. Minimum 2 Damage

## Giant Rat

- Hit Points: 1 +d6/2
- Evade: 6
- Limit 8
- Maximum 3 Damage
- When one rat dies, 1 nearby rat runs away...

## Giant Rat King

- Hit Points: 7 or more
- Evade: 5
- Limit: 11
- Whenever Rat King is under 6 HP, split apart into half that many Big Rats

## Wolf

- Hit Points: 2 + d6
- Evade: 5
- Limit: 10
- Attacks at Advantage when there is a Pack Leader nearby

## Wolf Pack Leader

- Hit Points: 4 +d6
- Evade: 5
- Limit: 10
- Attacks at Advantage
- Howls, costs Move, nearby Wolves Pocket Roll.

## Big Bat

- Hit Points: 1 +d6/3
- Evade: 6
- Limit: 8
- Maximum Damage 3
- Recovers its Move after an Attack

## Snake

- Hit Points: 1 + d6/2
- Evade: 6
- Limit: 10
- Maximum Damage 3
- On Hit mark a pip or take d6\* damage

## Bear

- Hit Points: 18 +d6
- Evade: 4
- Limit: 11
- Attacks Twice, Critical Hit +d6\*

## Fire Pig

- Hit Points: 24 +d6
- Evade 3, Limit 11
- Damage Reduction 1
- Tusk, attack with advantage, on Strong Hit attack again
- Charge, costs both Move and Action, Move and trample anyone in an adjacent location
- Fire Breath
  - Prepare, costs Move, ends turn. Next turn Breath Attack
  - Breath Attack, costs Action, anyone near rolls DC7 Dex or takes 3d6 damage
