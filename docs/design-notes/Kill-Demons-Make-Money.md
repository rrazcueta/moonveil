## Baby Oinker

- Hit Points: 18 +d6
- Evade: 4
- Limit: 11
- Attacks Twice, Critical Hit +d6

## Goblin

Clubs, slings, kitchen knives. Annoying. Comes in pairs.

- Hit Points: d6
- Evade: 6
- Limit: 10
- Makeshift Bomb, Range 1.
  - Roll d6, on 6 it explodes immediately and everyone takes d6 damage.
  - On 2-5 it explodes next round.
  - On 1 it's a dud

## Tall Goblin

- Hit Points: 9 +d6
- Evade: 5
- Limit: 10
- Throwing Spear - Strong Attack +1 damage, Range 1

## Ogre

- Hit Points: 12 + d6
- Evade: 4
- Limit: 10
- Swipe
  - Attack Roll against 2x
  - On Critical grabs 1st, damages 2nd d6
  - Grabbed characters
    - Roll at Disadvantage, 0 Evade
    - DC10 Anything to escape the grab
  - Throw Heavy, Strong Damage +Slots
- Damage Reduction 1

## Armored Ogre

- Hit Points: 15 + d6
- Evade: 4
- Limit: 11
- Heavy Slash,
  - Minimum 4
  - Strong Hit +d6 damage
- Swipe
  - Attack Roll against 2x
  - On Critical grabs 1st, damages 2nd d6
  - Grabbed characters
    - Roll at Disadvantage, 0 Evade
    - DC10 Anything to escape the grab
  - Throw Heavy, Strong Damage +Slots
- Damage Reduction 2

## Fire Pig

- Hit Points: 24 +d6
- Evade 3, Limit 11
- Damage Reduction 1
- Tusk, attack with advantage, on Strong Hit attack again
- Charge, costs both Move and Action, Move and trample anyone in an adjacent location
- Fire Breath
  - Prepare, costs Move, ends turn. Next turn Breath Attack
  - Breath Attack, costs Action, anyone near rolls DC7 Dex or takes 3d6 damage
