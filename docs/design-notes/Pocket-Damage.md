# Pocket Damage

I really want to get Pocket control right.

There previously were two ways to control pocket: Stunt/Stagger Attack (note to self: do not keep renaming things) and Finesse Gambit. Both were very similar but different cost - the Attack cost an Action and got the effect on Success, and the Gambit cost an Action plus risking all of your Dexterity to get the effect regardless of Success and then... you were rolling to keep your Dex. There were some other fiddly bits like the Move/Push effect, but the main goal always was Pocket manipulation.

Writing it out this way makes it kind of obvious that you can put them together. Current text:

```
### Finesse

Before a melee attack if you have a Pocket of 4 or more.

- Choose your target among nearby enemies.
- Depending on the Hit
  - Strong Hit, choose one effect
  - Critical Hit, choose two effects
- Effects to choose
  - The enemy rolls Pocket Down
  - Recover your Move OR force the enemy to Move 1
- If your attack roll is less than the enemy Limit, roll Pocket down.
```

This retains

- target ability
- 1/2 effect as before

The risk is very different than the other 3 Gambits. And I'm letting enemies take on this Gambit. It's not mirrored with the other ones, but assymmetry is interesting.
