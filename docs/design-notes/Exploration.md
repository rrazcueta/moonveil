# Exploration

One of the first ideas I had for MV was to have a fluid system for downtime. But I tried to give it rules like the encounters. I spread rules out wide to differnt types of creation to give it flavor, but it always felt flat.

This attempt is to make the rule pretty simple:

When you rest you can spend a resource to tell the table what you're interested in.

This is the kindling for the Guide to turn into quest hooks. Some players might not be interested in this kind of thing and will want the Guide to "make the world uninterested in them" which I understand. They can do that and then spend the next few sessions wandering around the bazaar looking for the exact sword they want. This is just a tool to diegetically get what's in the player's brain into the GM's brain.
