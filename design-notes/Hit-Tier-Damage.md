# Changes

- Defense is Min(Evade + Pocket, Limit -1)
- Strong Hit = Smaller Die

# Why

If Defense is always below Limit, then 9 is at least a Strong Hit.

There were two reasons I pushed Strong Hit be UNDER limit (as opposed to AT LIMIT)

- Having a Strong Hit band at all meant that Spears felt useful
- Clarity

I'm unsure exactly why it was unclear, but no one understood why Defense sat to the right of the Limit bar. It was always an issue, and it's just much easier this way. Average damages haven't changed much with this shift - presumably because the Defense change and the Strong Hit change offset each other (see attackTest2.ts) - so I think that works out.
